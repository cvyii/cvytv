## In-app playback rule

CvyTV plays a channel inside its own video player only when a channel record contains an authorized stream URL and the source is verified for the target browser/VIDAA playback environment. The official-page records above do not meet that condition. Do not add scraped IPTV lists, leaked HLS links, credentials, DRM bypasses, or redistributed broadcaster streams. To enable real in-app playback, obtain written permission or an official developer/player endpoint from each broadcaster, then verify codec, CORS, HLS/DASH/DRM support, and VIDAA compatibility on the exact Hisense model.
